How it works
  1. Add accounts by scanning a QR code, uploading an image or file, pasting a link, or typing the key.
  2. Each code changes when its ring runs out. Tap a card to copy the code.
  3. Everything stays on this device. Nothing is sent to a server.
  4. Turn on Encryption to lock your data and backups with your own key. A lost key cannot be recovered.
  5. Clearing browser data deletes your accounts, so download a backup.
