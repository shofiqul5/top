How to use

  1. Account. Pick a name and create your account. Back it up from ⋮ → Profile.
  2. ⋮ menu. New connect adds a friend by ID or QR. Profile has your photo, QR and backup. Settings has themes and help.
  3. Contact chat. Your friends. Search them, tap to chat, use the top icons to call.
  4. Install. Settings → Install app, or use Add to Home Screen in your browser.
  5. World chat. Find people and groups online. Tap + to create a group.
